# Erratum and notes to the PI-CAI prespecification (2026-11-12)

Written after the lock and after the marksheet was read for eligibility, but before any PI-CAI image
was opened and before any model output existed. None of these points changes an endpoint, a
threshold, the margin or the analysis population.

1. **Reference standard of negatives (correction).** The prespecification says PI-CAI labels
   "combine histopathology with radiological follow-up for some negatives". The labels repository
   (README at commit ce4a4723d7c46d882a6cbaacb40ed6c4be86282f) states otherwise: positives are
   histologically confirmed (ISUP >= 2); negatives are confirmed either histologically (ISUP <= 1)
   or by MRI alone (PI-RADS <= 2), without follow-up. The limitation should read: negatives without
   biopsy were defined by the MRI reading itself, so some clinically significant cancers may be
   missed among them (verification bias). This affects both PSA densities in the comparison.

2. **More AI segmentations in the repository (note).** At this commit the repository also holds
   AI-derived zonal segmentations (`zonal_pz_tz/AI/HeviAI23`, `zonal_pz_tz/AI/Yuan23`) and a second
   AI whole-gland set (`whole_gland/AI/Guerbet23`). They are not mentioned in the
   prespecification and are not used here. The statement that PI-CAI has no zonal reference
   segmentation stands, because these are AI-derived, not expert references.

3. **Comparison sets of the overlap check (clarification).** "PROSTATEx" means the 204 axial T2
   images used in this study (the Cuocolo et al. subset), not every PROSTATEx patient. "MSD" means
   the T2 channel of all images in `imagesTr` and `imagesTs` of Task05. "NCI-ISBI 2013" means the
   T2 images used in the study.
